Why This Mod?

Makes it much easier for your average joe to get a LAN world up and running without wifi, so playing together is still possible!

Offline Mode

Disables the mojang authentication for the SOLE purpose of letting a user join a world completely offline.

IP Sharing Made Easy

When opening a LAN world, the mod shows your ipv4 adress so your buddy can join easier!

Network helper

If you arent connected to a wifi network when opening to LAN, the mod gives you a lil heads up :D

Custom Offline Skins

Commonly, on offline servers, a grievance is being limited to the default skins. If you go to the mod config from mod menu, you can use a skin file from your device on servers OR Use one of the already loaded skins!

Wardrobe

Quickly swap between all your loaded skins/capes or equip one of the preloaded skins! (is there a better word in place of preloaded?)

How to Use

  1. Connect to the same network: Ensure all players are on the same Wi-Fi network (a mobile hotspot works perfectly fine, its how i usually use this.).
  2. Open your world: Start your single-player world in Minecraft.
  3. Open to LAN: Press Escape, click "Open to LAN". Keep the pre-filled settings unless you need to do something specific, but the prefilled settings are all you need.
  4. Share your IP: Start the LAN world. The mod will output a message in chat like Connect on 192.168.1.5 or 192.168.1.5:PORT.
  5. Friends Join: Your friends can go to Multiplayer -> Direct Connection, type in that exact IP address and port, and join the game!

Should i use it on the client?

    It is not Necessary for the mod to be on the client, but loading a custom skin & seeing other people's custom skins require the mod to be on the client.

    I also plan to add more features to the mod in the future that might require the client and server to talk to eachother!
